# Prompt 10 device test plan

Build using `JAVA_HOME=/opt/android-studio/jbr ./gradlew assembleDebug testDebugUnitTest assembleDebugAndroidTest --no-daemon --no-configuration-cache`. Install the debug APK. Live Firebase/profile/subscription and reminder tests require a signed-in device; compilation alone does not verify these external behaviors.

1. Open Profile. Check the circular initials avatar and editable name, phone and contact email. Submit invalid values and then valid edits. Verify the same UID's Firestore user fields and local session update without changing role or membership. Relaunch and confirm persistence. Sign in using the original Firebase Authentication email; contact-email editing does not change the login address, as explained in the UI.
2. Open My Cars, My Bookings and About. Back should return normally. Verify About describes the course demo without implying real payments or guaranteed diagnosis.
3. Disable appointment reminders, close/reopen Profile and restart. The preference must persist per user. Existing cached alarms should cancel and delivered stale intents should not post reminders. Re-enable and inspect future alarms with `adb shell dumpsys alarm`.
4. Open Plus. Confirm the ৳199/month pitch, explicit no-payment demo label and concrete benefits. Subscribe online, double-tap and rotate during the request. Verify `plus=true` and a `plusUntil` approximately 30 days ahead, without repeated extensions for an already-active membership. Check the active-until display and disabled Subscribe button.
5. Book a ৳1,000 service as a standard member: total ৳1,000 and Firestore commission ৳100. Subscribe, refresh/open a new booking: total ৳950, visible Plus discount, and stored commission ৳95. The customer must not see commission in the form or detail screen.
6. Compare slot lists: the opening-hour start is Plus-only, subject to actual availability and service duration. Confirm an active Plus member can reserve it and a standard/expired member cannot. Two customers must still not overlap an existing reservation. Existing reservations are not revoked by introducing priority eligibility.
7. Request free pickup as Plus. Verify `pickupRequested=true` and the detail label. A non-member must have the option disabled and cannot forge that entitlement through the repository write. Pickup is a workshop request, not actual dispatch.
8. Confirm a Plus booking over four hours in the future. Check its reminder is scheduled four hours before; standard bookings use two hours. Past trigger times are skipped. Reminder settings override both. A booking's snapshotted Plus benefit remains after subsequent membership expiry.
9. In a test account, set `plusUntil` to the past and refresh/relaunch. The UI and transaction must return to standard pricing/eligibility. Create a queued Plus booking offline, expire the membership before sync, reconnect: a stale quote must remain unsynced rather than silently charging more. Cancel and rebook after refresh.
10. Logout with future reminders and a running sync. Verify alarms/notifications are removed, the Service/listener stops, session clears, and Back cannot return to Profile/Home. Rotate during logout. Another account must not inherit the old account's reminder preference, Plus entitlement or cached rows.

No real payment, subscription renewal, pickup dispatch or Firebase console mutation is performed by the runner. Business rates and example calculations are documented in `docs/BUSINESS_MODEL.md`.
